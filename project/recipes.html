Brown Butter Chocolate Cookies

45 min Medium

View ingredients and steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter
  • 1 cup (200 g) brown sugar
  • 1 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 1/4 cups (280 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cups (225 g) chocolate chips or chunks

Steps

  1. Brown the butter: melt butter over medium heat; stir until amber and nutty (5 to 8 minutes). Cool 10 minutes
  2. Mix sugars into browned butter.
  3. Whisk in eggs and vanilla until glossy.
  4. Fold in flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined.
  5. Stir in chocolate.
  6. Stir in chocolate.
  7. Chill doug 30 minutes to reduce spreading.

Technique note

Cool the browned butter before adding eggs to prevent scrambling and greasy texture.

Store airttight 3 to 4 days. Freeze scooped dough balls for bake-from-frozen cookies.

Lemon Blueberry Loaf

70 min Easy

View ingredients and steps

Ingeredients

  • 1 1/2 cups (190 g) flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup (113 g) butter, softened (or neutral oil)
  • 3/4 cup (150 g) sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) milk or yogurt
  • 1 cup (150 g) blueberries (tossed with 1 tsp flour)

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line loaf pan
  2. Whisk dry ingredients
  3. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs, lemon zest, and juice
  4. Stir in milk/yogurt, then dry ingredients. Fold in blueberries.
  5. Bake 50 to 60 minutes until a tester comes out clean

Technique notes

Tossing berries with a little flour helps reduce sinking and streaking.

Cinnamon Rolls

2 hr 15 min Medium

View ingredients and steps

Ingredients

  • 3 1/4 cups (410 g) flour
  • 2 1/4 tsp yeast
  • 1/4 cup (50 g) sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup (240 ml) warm milk
  • 1/4 cup (57 g) melted butter
  • 1 egg

Fillngs

  • 1/3 cup (75 g) butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup (150 g) brown suggar
  • 1 1/2 tbsp cinnamon

Steps

  1. Mix dry ingredients. Add milk, butter, egg; knead until smooth (6 to 8 minutes)
  2. Rise covered until doubled (about 60 minutes).
  3. Roll into rectangle; spread butter; sprinkle fillling.
  4. Roll up, slice into 9 to 12 rolls; rise 30 to 45 minutes.
  5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 to 25 minutes.

Technique notes

Doug should feel and slightly tacky, not dry. Add flour sparingly while kneading.

No-Bake Cheesecake Cups

20 min + chill. Easy

View ingredients and steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (100 g) graham cracker crumbs
  • 3 tbsp (40 g) melted butter
  • 8 oz (225 g) cream cheese, softened
  • 1/3 cup (40 g) powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3/4 cup (180 ml) whipped heavy cream
  • Fruit or jam topping

Steps

  1. Mix crumbs and butter; press into cups.
  2. Beat cream cheese, powdered sugar, vanilla until smooth.
  3. Fold in whipped cream; portion into cups.
  4. Chill at leas 2 hours; top before serving.

Technique notes

Whip cream to soft peaks for a lighter texture, then fold gently.

Chocolate Ganache Tart

60 min + chill Medium

View ingredients and steps

Ingredients (Crust)

  • 1 1/4 cups (16o g) flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder (optional)
  • 1/4 cup (30 g) powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cup (113 g) cold butter, cubed
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 to 3 tbsp cold water

Ganache

  • 8 oz (225 g) semi-sweet chocolate, chopped
  • 1 cup (240 ml) heavy cream
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Make crust; press into tart pan; chill 45 minutes
  2. Bake 350°F (175°C) for 18 to 22 minutes; cool.
  3. Heat cream until streaming; pour over chocolate and salt; stirt smooth.
  4. Pour into crust; chill until set (about 2 hours).

Technique notes

For a glossy ganache, let the hot cream sit on the chocolate for 2 minutes before stirringl.

Banana Bread

70 min Easy

View ingredients and steps

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup (113 g) melted butter (or oil)
  • 3/4 cup (150 g) sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 1/2 cups (190 g) flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Nuts or chocolate chips optional

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a loaf pan.
  2. Mix bananas, butter, sugar; add eggs and vanilla.
  3. Fold in flour, baking soda, and salt just until combined.
  4. Bake 50 to 60 minutes; cool before slicing.

Technique notes

Use very ripe bananas for better sweetness abnd aroma.

French Crepes

30 min + rest Easy

View ingredients and steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (125 g) flours
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/4 cups (300 ml) milk
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp vanilla

Steps

  1. Whisk flour and salt. Add eggs.
  2. Gradually whisk in milk until smooth; whisk in melted butter.
  3. Rest batter 15 minutes (better texture). Optional: strain for smoothness.
  4. Cook thin crepes in a lightly buttered pan; flip once edges set.

Technique notes

Use medium heat. If crepes brown too fast, reduce heat and wipe pan lightly with butter.

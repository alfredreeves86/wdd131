Guides & Baking Tips

Technique-first notes to help your bakes come out consistent.

Precision basics

Measuring flour

  • Best: use a kitchen scale (grams).
  • If using cups: spoon flour into the cuo and level; don't pack.

Ingredients Temperature

  • Room-temp butter and eggs help batters emulsify smoothly.
  • Cold ingredients can cause curdling and uneven rise.

Baking soda vs baking powder

  • Baking soda: needs acid (yogurt, lemon, brown sugar) and helps browning.
  • Baking powder: includes acid, lifts more predictably in neutral batters.

Cream butter and sugar until ligther in color and fluffy, usually 2 to 4 minutes. This builds structure and fine crumb.

Troubleshooting

Common issues and quick fixes.

Cookies spread too much

  • Chill dough 20 to 30 minutes.
  • Make sure butter isn't too hot (especially after browning).
  • Use a cool baking sheet for each batch

Cupcakes are dry

  • Reduce bake time slightly; pull when just set.
  • Measure flour carefully (too much flour dries baked goods).
  • Store airttight as soon as fully cooled.

Quick tips

Short reminders you can use while baking.

  • Oven rack: middle rack for even heat unless recipe says otherwise.
  • Prep: cakes spring back lightly, edges pull from pan, tester comes out clean.
